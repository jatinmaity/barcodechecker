Barcode tag checker

Upload the tag artwork and the PO barcode Excel. Every barcode is scanned and every tag is read and compared with its Excel row. Your files stay on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

Text on the tags is read by OCR. If a single field is flagged on one tag but looks right in the picture, it is most likely a misread; recheck it by eye. Barcode values and PDF quantities are read exactly.
